How to play

Miners spread across crystal patches and bring crystals home. Tap a patch to see its remaining reserve. Build another command base near a fresh field to shorten mining trips. Tap your command base to train more. Build a factory for Strikers, and a Tech core for siege bots plus timed weapons and armor research. Select your base or factory, press Rally, then tap where new units should go. Choose Easy for more preparation time, or Hard for earlier attacks. Select Army, then tap a destination to move and fight, or tap an enemy to focus fire. In Army controls, Select + lets you add or remove units by tapping them. Save two squads and recall them with Squad 1 / 2 (or keyboard 1 / 2; Shift saves). Tap a battle alert to jump to trouble. Drag the battlefield or tap the minimap to explore. Pink crystals yield more, but damage nearby units during their pulse. Destroy every enemy command base to win. Matches have a twenty-minute limit.
