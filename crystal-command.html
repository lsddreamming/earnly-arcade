How to play

Miners automatically bring crystals home. Tap your command base to train more. Build a factory for Strikers, and a Tech core for siege bots. Select Army, then tap a destination to move and fight. Drag the battlefield or tap the minimap to explore. Pink crystals yield more, but damage nearby units during their pulse. Destroy every enemy command base to win.
