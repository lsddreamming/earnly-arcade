❓ Common Questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play Earnly Arcade as a guest. Signing in is optional and is used for cloud backup and syncing progress between devices.

What are Arcade Coins?

Arcade Coins are in-app reward points earned through gameplay and challenges. In the current version, Arcade Coins do not have a cash value and cash-out is not enabled.

How do free plays work?

Each game includes free plays. When you run out, available bonus play options may appear. Free plays refill according to the limits shown inside Earnly.

How do I save my progress?

Open Profile → Account & Data and sign in. Earnly Cloud can then back up Coins, XP, scores, missions, and other progress automatically.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Open Profile → Account & Data, choose “Forgot password?”, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to you.

A game or reward is not working correctly.

Close and reopen Earnly, confirm your internet connection, and try again. If the problem continues, email support with the game name and what happened.

🔐 Account & Safety

Earnly support will never ask for your password. For account backup, password recovery, or restoring progress, use the tools inside Account & Data.
