Harbor Getaway

3:00ON FOOT Find the marked getaway car.

After hours at the harbor

Grab the mint coupe, break the patrol’s line of sight, then drive it back to the garage. You have three minutes.

Drag the view to look around. Use WASD or arrows to move and drive, Q/R to look, E to enter or exit, Space for handbrake, and Esc to pause. On touch, hold ↑ and a steering arrow together to turn while driving.
