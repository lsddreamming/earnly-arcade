SURVIVAL FIELD MANUAL

Take them apart.

  1. Aim with the camera. Your robot faces where you look. Get close and strike across an exposed side to remove an arm.
  2. Watch the red ring. A robot is winding up. Dodge out, or tap block just before impact for a parry.
  3. Three swing combo. Hold strike for left, right, then a vertical chop. Jump above a swing. Dodge through danger using energy.
  4. Break the machine. Missing legs slow robots down. Missing arms take weapons away. Break the head or core to finish them.
  5. Rebuild between waves. Pick one upgrade. Every fifth wave brings the Crucible giant.
