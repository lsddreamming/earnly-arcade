Before you start

Choose a game below or browse the arcade. Use the game’s Start button or start area, then wait for its countdown. Pause and Quit are next to the game controls; confirm Quit to leave. A results screen shows your score and earned progress when a run finishes.

You can play as a guest. Each game has its own reward rules and limits; the game and results screen show the current details. Arcade Coins are in-app points, with no cash-out or real-value redemption available. See Rewards for more.

Neon Breach

A first-person arena where robot waves pressure you to move and aim at the same time.

Controls

Use the movement pad to move, drag to aim, and hold Fire. On a keyboard, use WASD to move, arrows to aim, and Space to fire.

Goal and challenge

Survive the robot waves while protecting your health. Higher waves hit harder.

A useful strategy

Keep moving between shots and use the radar to find threats before they surround you.

Tap Rush

A short reaction challenge built around accurate taps under a shrinking clock.

Controls

Tap the green target. Each miss costs 0.75 seconds from the 20-second run.

Goal and challenge

Land as many correct hits as you can before time runs out.

A useful strategy

Watch where the next target appears. A deliberate hit is better than several hurried misses.

Neon Dodger

A neon traffic challenge with districts, collectible cores, and car unlocks.

Controls

Drag your finger to steer, or use Left/Right arrows or A/D on a keyboard.

Goal and challenge

Avoid collisions, collect cores, and chain close passes. Five cores in a row trigger Overdrive; missing a core breaks the streak.

A useful strategy

Choose a safe escape route before chasing a core. Close passes require steering, so parking in one spot will not build that bonus.

Snake

Grow a neon snake by collecting apples while keeping a route open for its expanding body.

Controls

Swipe to steer, use the direction buttons, or use arrow keys on a keyboard.

Goal and challenge

Eat apples without hitting walls or your own body. One Coin is earned every three apples, with milestone bonuses and a 30-Coin run cap.

A useful strategy

Turn before you reach an edge and leave space to escape. Longer bodies make a tempting apple harder to reach safely.

Star Defender

An alien-wave shooter where your ship fires automatically and your attention goes to dodging.

Controls

Drag to steer; keyboard players can use Left/Right arrows or A/D.

Goal and challenge

Defeat aliens and avoid incoming shots. Three hits end the run, and a run lasts at most 45 seconds.

A useful strategy

Move toward open space between shots rather than chasing every alien across the screen.

Brick Breaker

Clear changing brick patterns with a paddle and ball, then face tougher boards.

Controls

Drag left or right to move the paddle and keep it under the ball.

Goal and challenge

Clear the bricks and advance through levels. Later boards add armored bricks, faster balls, and a smaller paddle.

A useful strategy

Track the ball on its way down. Catch useful falling power-ups only when you can still return the ball safely.

Memory Match

A timed card challenge that rewards remembering positions and avoiding repeated mismatches.

Controls

Tap two cards at a time to reveal them. Matching pairs stay face-up.

Goal and challenge

Find all eight pairs in 60 seconds. The first two mismatches are free; each later mismatch costs four seconds.

A useful strategy

Explore systematically, then use remembered matches. Reopening a known mismatch wastes both time and moves.

Coin Catch

Catch falling Coins while avoiding bombs as the levels become more demanding.

Controls

Move the basket with your finger or mouse, or use Left/Right arrows or A/D.

Goal and challenge

A missed Coin adds one strike; catching a bomb adds two. Five strikes end the run, and strikes carry across levels.

A useful strategy

Prioritize avoiding bombs. Letting one Coin fall can be safer than moving into a bomb to catch it.

Jungle Hopper

Guide a hopping character through vine gaps with a rhythm of short taps.

Controls

Keep tapping to hop upward and let the character fall between taps.

Goal and challenge

Pass through the gaps without crashing.

A useful strategy

Use smaller corrections near a gap. A last-second burst of taps can lift you into the upper vine.

Block Drop

A falling-piece puzzle where complete horizontal rows clear space for the next pieces.

Controls

Drag left or right to move, tap to rotate, and drag down to drop. The buttons below the board also move, rotate, and drop pieces.

Goal and challenge

Fill full rows. The run ends when a new piece cannot fit.

A useful strategy

Watch the next piece and keep the surface fairly level. Deep holes become harder to fill as the board rises.

Tower Stack

Build a tower by dropping each moving floor onto the stack below it.

Controls

Tap when the moving floor lines up with the tower.

Goal and challenge

Stack as high as possible while keeping enough overlap for the next floor.

A useful strategy

Use the tower edge as your timing reference. Small alignment errors make later drops less forgiving.

Color Match

A quick decision game where the written word tries to distract you from its ink color.

Controls

Tap the button matching the ink color you see. Keyboard players can use 1, 2, 3, or 4 for the four buttons.

Goal and challenge

If RED is painted blue, choose BLUE. The ink color is the rule; the word is the distraction.

A useful strategy

Focus on the color first, then find its button. Reading the word automatically is the trap.

Paddle Rally

Keep a solo rally alive as each successful return makes the ball faster.

Controls

Move your finger or mouse to control the paddle, or use arrow keys.

Goal and challenge

Return the ball repeatedly. The run ends when it gets past your paddle.

A useful strategy

Start moving toward the landing position early. Large corrections are harder when the ball is close to the paddle.

Lane Runner

A lane-switching traffic run that asks you to choose openings before cars close them.

Controls

Swipe left or right to change lanes, or use Left/Right arrows on a keyboard.

Goal and challenge

Dodge oncoming cars as traffic becomes faster and more frequent.

A useful strategy

Look beyond the nearest car. A lane that is clear now may have another obstacle arriving immediately behind it.

Safe Cracker

Stop a moving marker inside the green target to open a series of increasingly difficult locks.

Controls

Tap to stop the marker while it is inside the green zone.

Goal and challenge

Successful locks shrink the target and speed up the marker. Three misses end the run; waiting too long also costs a life.

A useful strategy

Follow one pass to judge the speed, then commit. Waiting indefinitely will not protect the run.

Neon Drift

Slide through neon road bends and build combos with close passes.

Controls

Hold the left or right drift control and release to straighten. Keyboard players can use Left/Right arrows or A/D.

Goal and challenge

Follow the road, avoid traffic, and chain close passes for a stronger combo.

A useful strategy

Release before oversteering. Set up for the next bend instead of reacting only when you reach its edge.

Merge Rush

A limited-swipe number puzzle with targets to clear rather than an endless score chase.

Controls

Swipe in a direction to slide the board. Matching numbers merge.

Goal and challenge

Make 64 in 48 swipes and clear the displayed targets to score.

A useful strategy

Keep larger tiles together and plan where new merges will land. Random swipes use up the move budget.

Perfect Drop

A precision timing game where every drop must land inside the green target.

Controls

Tap to drop when the moving piece is aligned with the target.

Goal and challenge

Build your hit count and advance levels. Level five starts at 20 hits; three misses end the run.

A useful strategy

Judge the movement before tapping. Keep aiming for the center as the challenge grows.

Controls

Move left or right to line up the ball with each opening.

Goal and challenge

Clear rows by finding the gaps as they become tighter.

A useful strategy

Look at the opening below the current row and begin lining up before the ball reaches it.

Shape Fit

A visual matching challenge where the correct silhouette may be shown in another direction.

Controls

Choose the option with the same shape as the target, even when it is rotated.

Goal and challenge

Answer before the timer expires. Three mistakes end the run.

A useful strategy

Compare distinctive corners and proportions. Orientation alone does not make a shape different.

Bounce Run

An obstacle runner built around well-timed jumps as the pace increases.

Controls

Tap anywhere in the play area to jump.

Goal and challenge

Clear the red obstacles and keep the run going as it gets faster.

A useful strategy

Watch the spacing between obstacles. The right timing for one jump may leave you badly placed for the next.

Traffic Escape

Clear a crowded road board by choosing cars whose arrow paths are open.

Controls

Tap a car only when the path in the direction of its arrow is clear.

Goal and challenge

Each cleared road raises density and tightens the timer. Three crashes end the run.

A useful strategy

Open space from the outside inward. Check the entire arrow path before tapping a car in the middle of traffic.

Play Traffic EscapeBack to guide list ↑

Neon Maze

Collect energy cells through maze sectors while evading sentry drones.

Controls

Swipe to steer continuously, hold a direction button, or use arrow keys or WASD.

Goal and challenge

Clear sectors to add 35 seconds, up to a 120-second time bank. Three sentry hits end the run.

A useful strategy

Plan a route through nearby cells and leave yourself a way around the drones instead of heading into a dead end.
