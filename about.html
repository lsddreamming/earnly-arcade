About Earnly Arcade

Short games. Fresh challenges. A new personal best to chase.

Explore the gamesLearn how to play

An arcade you can open anywhere

Earnly Arcade brings together quick reaction games, timing challenges, puzzles, driving games, and a first-person robot arena. Play in your browser on a phone or computer. You can try games as a guest; an account is optional for cloud saves and a public leaderboard identity.

The arcade uses a neon style and short runs. Some games ask for accurate taps, some need careful steering, and others reward planning. Choose from the Games page, use its search and category filters, or let “Pick for me” choose your next run.

Find your kind of challenge

For a quick reaction test, try Tap Rush. For a longer skill chase, Snake builds pressure as your body grows, while Neon Drift adds faster sectors and collectible Coins. Neon Breach combines movement and aiming against robot waves.

If you prefer thinking before acting, Merge Rush gives you a limited swipe budget, and Memory Match challenges you to recall card positions against the clock. The Game Guides explain every game’s controls, objective, and a useful starting strategy.

Plays, Coins, and XP

Each game offers three free plays, with a daily refill. Optional rewarded ads can unlock extra plays when available. Ad availability can vary; watching an ad does not directly award Arcade Coins.

Gameplay and challenges award Arcade Coins and XP. XP raises your player level. Coins are in-app points, and eligible game features such as Continue may spend them. Coins are not cash or cryptocurrency and cannot be transferred, withdrawn, or redeemed for real-value rewards in the current version.

Check the game’s reward information and results screen for its current rules. The Rewards page explains your balance and the difference between Coins, XP, and plays.

Progress you can come back to

Personal bests, daily and weekly missions, achievements, and streaks give you new goals beyond a single score. Guests keep progress in their browser or device. Sign in through Account & Data to use cloud backup and restore supported progress across devices.

Sound, motion, haptics, and text-size options are in Settings. You can add the website to your Home Screen from a supported browser for quick access.

A growing arcade shaped by playtesting

Earnly Arcade is actively being developed and tested. Games and difficulty are refined using player feedback, and features can change as the arcade improves. Real-value redemption is not live; possible future reward options are still being evaluated.

Found confusing controls, a broken screen, or a game that needs work? Use Help & Contact. Include the game name, your device or browser, and what happened. Your feedback helps identify what to improve next.
