Harbor sound credits

Recordings are trimmed, converted to mono 22.05 kHz MP3, faded and mixed at reduced volume. Engine pitch follows vehicle speed. Short vocal reactions are stylized, not recordings of real injury.

  • Engine: Racing car engine sound loops by domasx2, CC0. loop_0.wav, originally adapted from a public-domain pdsounds recording.
  • Gunshot: Gunshots by kurt, CC0. 22 Pistol.wav; one short shot excerpt.
  • Vocal reaction: High pitch scream sounds by pauliuw, CC0. high_pitch_scream.mp3; short excerpt, reduced volume and varied pitch.
  • Tire braking: Car tire squeal / skid loop. Original recording by audible-edge (Tom Haigh), loop edit by qubodup. Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported. tires_squal_loop.wav; resampled, encoded, faded and volume reduced. No endorsement implied.
