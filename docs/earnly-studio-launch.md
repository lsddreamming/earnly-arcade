# Earnly Studio launch economics and release criteria

**Pricing review · November 27, 2026 · USD.** Start private testing with FLUX.1 Schnell for images and Wan Fast 5B for short silent text-to-video. Prioritize finished media quality on an iPhone, the cost of an acceptable result, and the full time from submission to playback. The figures below are published list prices and operating scenarios. Earnly has yet to measure provider invoices, throughput, ad income, moderation cost, or creator retention for Studio.

Record each enabled endpoint, accepted commercial terms, effective date, processing locations, output-use conditions, attribution, media retention, rights to the input, and permitted content. **Private qualification run.** Use original prompts covering motion, comedy, landscape, fast action, cartoons and vertical framing, plus an owned Spuddy image. Submit six FLUX images, six 480p Fast clips, six 720p Fast clips and three standard image animations. Expected raw inference is $0.693 with one billed megapixel per image. Start serially with a $1 initial test allocation inside the owner's $25 lifetime ceiling. Verify returned dimensions, decoded duration, audio presence, safety handling and playback on a physical iPhone. Record billable units and exact invoice amounts, queue delay, processing time, end-to-end duration, download size and acceptance after blind review. Report median and range for a small sample, and collect more data before claiming p95 performance or a reliability SLA. Disable automatic top-ups and reconcile billing even after client timeouts. ## Full-allowance monthly contribution

The base case assumes every included image uses one billed megapixel and every video uses the 720p Fast text model. The allowance figures below are financial proposals. Checkout and entitlement configuration require separate verification.

| Full consumption per subscriber | Raw inference | Modeled operating cost | Estimated iOS contribution | Estimated US web contribution |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Premium at $9.99, 300 images + 15 Fast clips | $1.28 | $2.80 | $3.90 (39%) | $6.23 (62%) |
| Requested Pro at $19.99, 1,000 images + 40 Fast clips | $4.00 | $7.72 | $5.67 (28%) | $10.65 (53%) |
| Proposed starting Pro at $19.99, 700 images + 35 Fast clips | $2.98 | $5.86 | $7.53 (38%) | $12.51 (63%) |

Contribution is after modeled variable costs and fees as a percentage of gross customer price. It excludes income tax, any sales tax included in displayed prices, fixed engineering, paid acquisition and exceptional support or moderation incidents. Use actual net-of-tax proceeds by storefront before selling in that country. Assumptions:

- Reserve 15% extra inference for charged outputs that cannot be delivered, moderation rejections, and recoverable failures. A single submission may fail after the provider has finished and billed it.
- Allow 1 MB per image and 10 MB per video, three months of storage and five complete uncached downloads. Use $0.0213/GB-month and $0.09/GB egress. Longer retention needs a new forecast and a stated retention policy. Public-feed views can multiply egress independently of generation, so introduce a separate playback budget, thumbnails and lazy loading. [Storage](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/pricing) · [Egress](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/egress)
- Reserve $0.50/account-month plus $0.0015/image and $0.01/video for moderation, job execution and support allocation. These are provisional allowances. Measure actual moderation services and staffed review time before billing.
- Use a 30% Apple commission stress case and a 3% of gross refund/financial reserve on both channels. For US domestic cards model 2.9% + $0.30 plus 0.7% subscription billing. Do not count a Small Business rate until enrollment is confirmed. Cross-border, tax tooling and dispute fees can change the outcome. [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/programs/whats-included/) · [Stripe Payments](https://stripe.com/pricing) · [Stripe Billing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing)

At $0.15 per standard animation, substituting image-to-video for every included Fast clip reduces Premium iOS contribution to $1.74 and the original Pro proposal to approximately **−$0.08**. Use separate image and Fast video quotas, or enforce model-weighted debits calibrated against the most expensive redemption path. For a first approximation charge six Fast video allowances for one standard Wan animation. Higher-cost audio models require their own rate card. Present the actual credit cost before submission, including an explicit price change if a quote expires. Credit packs require the same worst-case redemption calculation and the channel's fixed fee. Keep permanent purchased units separately from period-limited service allowances.

## Rewarded ads and promotions

At a hypothetical $3 in realized publisher revenue per 1,000 rewarded completions, Earnly receives $0.003 per completion. This is a sensitivity input, not Earnly revenue data. Reconcile net revenue against valid *rewarded completions* for that placement, channel and geography, since served impressions can differ from successful grants.

| Reward | Raw inference break-even per 1,000 rewards | Break-even including modeled variable processing, storage, and failed-output reserve |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| One basic image | $3.00 | $5.46 |
| Three basic images | $9.00 | $16.39 |
| One 480p Fast video | $12.50 | $29.51 |
| One 720p Fast video | $25.00 | $43.89 |

These thresholds exclude the subscriber-level $0.50 support allowance and any desired margin. Require realized income above break-even with a buffer. Start the free promotion budget at zero. A bounded promotional pool can finance an occasional highlighted creation independently of ad yield. When a pool cannot afford the promise, hide the offer **before** requesting an ad. Show the exact nontransferable Studio reward and an ordinary dismiss option. Verified ad completions may accumulate partial credits once economics and policy permit the proposed redemption and downloadable outputs. No rewards for clicking an ad. [Google reward policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7313578)

Use AdMob's signed SSV query and current Google verification keys. Bind an expiring one-time Studio grant intent to the account, placement, ad unit, amount and revenue configuration. Uniquely record the provider's transaction ID and grant once in the same database transaction. Treat client dismissal/completion as UI state only. Capture separate evidence for local fixtures, a signed Google test callback, native test ads and a real device/placement callback. Do not generate live self-impressions for testing. [Google SSV](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/ssv) · [iOS rewarded ads](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/rewarded)

## Evidence required before enabling a release surface

| Surface | Release evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Private paid inference | Dedicated prepaid key with auto-refill off, reconciled starting balance and cumulative $25 ceiling across all days and provider accounts. Lock per-job maximum exposure before submission. Verify price units against the allowlist and reject changes or stale prices. Keep uncertain dispatches reserved for reconciliation. Exercise the emergency switch during queued and in-flight work. |
| Shared accounts and upload | Reuse Earnly authentication. Review explicit permission before transmitting personal data to third-party AI under Apple 5.1.2(i). Explain which processor receives what, why and how long media is kept. Offer withdrawal and deletion. For early tests use original fictional prompts or company-owned Spuddy assets. Keep uploads disabled until input validation, provenance/likeness controls, moderation and object authorization are working. |
| Private creations | Quarantine outputs until content checks complete, combine video frame and audio checks, verify storage MIME and size, keep a private bucket and short-lived signed reads. Test deletion through thumbnails, shared links, cached media and provider-side expiry. Document any required abuse or accounting record retention. |
| Paid plans and packs | Independent Studio product identifiers and subscription group. Verify Apple signed transaction, environment, bundle, product and account binding. Implement restoration, renewal, grace, cancellation at expiry, refund, revocation and replay/out-of-order notifications. Keep purchased credits non-expiring. Native checkout uses StoreKit. Web checkout verifies Stripe webhook signatures, canonical subscription/invoice status, identity, and refund/dispute events. Test server-side idempotency, including restore on another device. Never credit from a return URL. [Apple purchases](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase) · [Apple server notifications](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreservernotifications) · [Stripe subscription events](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/webhooks) |

Keep all community writes unavailable until a staffed review and escalation path has been tested. [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) |
