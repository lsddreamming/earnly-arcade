Coins, XP & plays explained

Coins unlock eligible items and extras. XP raises your level. Plays let you start games.

Some in-game features can spend Coins. Choose your items in the shop before purchasing.

Arcade Coins can be earned from games, daily bonuses, and daily challenges, or bought through available coin packs. Available rewarded ads offer voluntary +1-play unlocks or verified daily Coin bonuses.

Signed-in progress can sync to Earnly Cloud, and Coin rewards are reconciled against the secure server ledger.

Arcade Coins are in-app points. They can be used for eligible in-game features such as Continue, but they are not cash, cryptocurrency, or stored value. They have no fixed cash value and cannot be redeemed, transferred, or withdrawn in this version. Purchased coins are never redeemable for cash or cash equivalents.
