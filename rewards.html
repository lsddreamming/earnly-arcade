How Arcade Coins work

Arcade Coins come from game rewards, daily bonuses, and daily challenges. Rewarded ads offer voluntary +1-play unlocks, not Coins.

Signed-in progress can sync to Earnly Cloud, and Coin rewards are reconciled against the secure server ledger.

Arcade Coins are in-app points. They are not cash, cryptocurrency, or stored value. They have no fixed cash value and cannot be redeemed, transferred, or withdrawn in this version. Future reward terms may change before launch.
