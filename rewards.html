How rewards work right now

Arcade Coins come from game rewards, daily bonuses, and daily challenges. Rewarded ads only unlock extra game plays; they do not directly award Coins.

The prototype stores progress on this device for now, and Coins cannot be withdrawn yet. Before launch, the final conversion must be based on sustainable revenue, costs, payout rules, and fraud protection.
