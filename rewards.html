How Arcade Coins work

Arcade Coins come from game rewards, daily bonuses, and daily challenges. Rewarded ads unlock extra plays, not Coins.

Signed-in progress can sync to Earnly Cloud, and Coin rewards are reconciled against the secure server wallet. Gift card and Bitcoin redemption are planned but are not available in this version.

Earnly's target is to fund the future player reward pool with roughly 25%–50% of net ad revenue. The final percentage, Coin value, minimum redemption, and available payout methods may change before launch.
