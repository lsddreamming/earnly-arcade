How Arcade Coins work

Arcade Coins come from game rewards, daily bonuses, and daily challenges. Rewarded ads unlock extra plays, not Coins.

Signed-in progress can sync to Earnly Cloud, and Coin rewards are reconciled against the secure server wallet. Cash redemption is not available in this version.
