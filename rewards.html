How Arcade Coins work

Arcade Coins come from game rewards, daily bonuses, and daily challenges. Rewarded ads unlock extra plays, not Coins.

Signed-in progress can sync to Earnly Cloud, and Coin rewards are reconciled against the secure server ledger. Future reward options are being evaluated but are not available in this version.

Arcade Coins have no fixed cash value in this version. Future reward availability, eligibility, redemption rates, minimums, regions, and payout methods may change before launch.
