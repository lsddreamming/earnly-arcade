Before you start

Choose a game below or browse the arcade. Use the game’s Start button or start area, then wait for its countdown. Pause and Quit are next to the game controls; confirm Quit to leave. A results screen shows your score and earned progress when a run finishes.

You can play as a guest. Each game has its own reward rules and limits; the game and results screen show the current details. Arcade Coins are in-app points, with no cash-out or real-value redemption available. See Rewards for more.

Neon Slice

Controls

Swipe or drag through cookout food. On a keyboard, use arrows to move the blade and hold Space to slice. Pause and Quit are below the arena.

Goal and challenge

Food scores 10 points, and rare golden burgers score 50. Slice several items in one stroke for a combo bonus. Three misses or one bomb ends the run. Waves grow faster as you survive.

A useful strategy

Wait for food to rise, then swipe across the group. Avoid the bombs with glowing fuses. Signed-in players earn 1 Arcade Coin per 50 verified points, up to 30 per run and three rewarded runs per UTC day. Practice is unlimited; ad continues are score-only. Rounds last up to three minutes. Your best score stays on this device.

Crystal Command

Controls

Select troops by tapping or dragging a selection box, then tap the battlefield to move and attack. Use Build to place structures and Train to recruit units. Drag the map to look around; Pause and Quit sit below the controls.

Goal and challenge

Destroy every enemy structure to win. Completed wins lasting at least one minute with at least one enemy defeated earn 10 Coins plus one per three defeated units, up to 25 Coins. Lessons, quits, surrenders, draws and disconnects do not award Coins. Sign in to collect; up to nine rewarded battles per UTC day. Battles remain free to play.

Spend your Arcade Coins on avatar gear or furniture for your World rooms. Both use the same signed-in wallet.

A useful strategy

Protect your miners, scout enemy defenses, and combine ground troops with air support. Add supply buildings before your army fills its troop slots.

Neon Breach

A first-person arena where robot waves pressure you to move and aim at the same time.

Controls

Use the movement pad to move, drag to aim, and hold Fire. On a keyboard, use WASD to move, arrows to aim, and Space to fire.

Goal and challenge

Survive the robot waves while protecting your health. Higher waves hit harder.

A useful strategy

Keep moving between shots and use the radar to find threats before they surround you.

Tap Rush

Hit as many randomly placed green circles as possible before time runs out.

Controls

Tap or click the green circle. Each successful hit moves it immediately to a new random position. The circle stays until you hit it.

Goal and challenge

Start with 20 seconds. Every tap on the dark background removes 0.75 seconds without scoring. Pause freezes the clock; Quit is below the board. Your personal best and world ranking use targets hit.

A useful strategy

Follow the circle instead of repeatedly tapping one spot. Accuracy keeps more time on the clock. Earn Coins from hits and milestones, up to 60 per run.

Snake

Controls

Eat apples without hitting walls or your own body. One Coin is earned every three apples, with milestone bonuses and a 30-Coin run cap.

Star Defender

An alien-wave shooter where your ship fires automatically and your attention goes to dodging.

Controls

Drag to steer; keyboard players can use Left/Right arrows or A/D.

Goal and challenge

Defeat varied alien formations and the commander every fourth wave. Every five defeats grants rapid fire or a one-hit shield. Three unshielded hits end the run; each sortie lasts at most 45 seconds.

A useful strategy

Move toward open space and watch the firing markers below enemies. Complete daily flight goals and unlock four ship colors by defeating aliens across runs. These cosmetic goals are saved for this player on this device and do not grant extra coins.

Tower Stack

Build a tower by dropping each moving floor onto the stack below it.

Controls

Tap when the moving floor lines up with the tower.

Goal and challenge

Stack as high as possible while keeping enough overlap for the next floor.

A useful strategy

Safe Cracker

Time your tap to stop the white pin inside green. The gold center is an optional perfect hit.

Controls

Tap the playfield or Crack button. Keyboard players can use Space or Enter. Pause and Quit stay below the playfield.

Goal and challenge

Three mistakes end the run. Steady, Surge and Pulse patterns are labeled before each lock. Every tenth door needs two clean pins; a mistake resets that door. Four clean locks earn one bonus lock. Each pin offers at least two full traversals and a fair timing window.

A useful strategy

Watch one pass to learn the labeled pattern, then aim for the middle of green. Surge speeds up across the rail and Pulse changes pace smoothly. On two-pin vaults, wait for the second pin to become ready before tapping again. Protect your three lives; a clean green hit is enough to keep your streak.

Arcade Coin rewards

Earn 2 Coins per 5 locks, plus milestones at 25, 50, 75 and 100 locks. A 33-lock run earns 14 Coins; the 100-Coin run maximum is reached at 200 locks. Perfect pins and visual effects do not multiply coin rewards. Existing balances and high scores remain saved.

Neon Drift

Slide through neon road bends and build combos with close passes.

Controls

Hold the left or right drift control and release to straighten. Keyboard players can use Left/Right arrows or A/D.

Goal and challenge

Race through six progressively faster sectors. Collect gold Coins for a bonus, and collect five in a row for a four-second Overdrive boost. Clean sectors add two bonus Coins. Score Coins are capped at 25 and bonus Coins at 20 per completed run. Reaching sector three unlocks violet paint; sector five unlocks gold paint for future runs.

A useful strategy

Release before oversteering. Set up for the next bend and choose a safe route before chasing a Coin. Missing a pickup or crashing breaks your pickup streak. Bonuses settle once at the end of your run; Quit does not cash them in.

Perfect Drop

A precision timing game where every drop must land inside the green target.

Controls

Tap the playfield or the large Drop button to release the orb. Use Space or Enter on a keyboard. Each tap commits one drop; Pause and Quit stay below the controls.

Goal and challenge

Land the whole orb inside green. The gold center is a perfect landing. Three consecutive perfects charge cosmetic Skyfire effects. Each successful drop still scores exactly one hit. Three misses or missed deadlines end the run. Reach 20 hits for Level 5, then chase higher medals and your personal best.

A useful strategy

Watch the launch rail and tap while the orb lines up with green. Its horizontal position locks as soon as you release it. Pulse rounds change speed smoothly; every round provides at least two full catch opportunities and a fair timing window. Skyfire and medals add no Coins. Existing Arcade Coin reward milestones and the 100-Coin maximum remain unchanged.

Spiral Drop

Guide a falling ball through openings in successive rows.

Controls

Drag across the shaft or the steering pad below it. A new touch anchors to your current position, so the orb never jumps to your finger. Use Left and Right arrows on a keyboard. Pause and Quit stay below the steering pad.

Goal and challenge

Descend through the illuminated gaps without hitting a platform. Each cleared row scores one. Center three consecutive drops to activate Flow effects; Flow does not multiply your score or Coins. New zones open every 25 rows. Depth medals and your personal best give you the next target.

A useful strategy

Watch the next opening and begin your slide early. Aim for the center, leaving room for the entire orb to pass through. Every generated route leaves time to react and steer, even at maximum speed. Existing Arcade Coin milestones and the 100-Coin maximum are unchanged.

Traffic Escape

Untangle a city traffic puzzle across seven districts, from Harbor Start to City Center.

Controls

Tap or click a car when its entire arrow path is clear. Keyboard players can Tab between cars and press Enter or Space. Pause and Quit stay below the board.

Goal and challenge

Clear all cars before each road timer expires. Later districts contain more traffic and less time. Three blocked taps end the run; remaining chances carry across districts. Every generated road has a complete solution. Each departing car scores exactly one, up to 59 cars across seven districts.

A useful strategy

Trace each arrow to the edge, then work backward through cars blocking it. Colors identify vehicles and do not reveal safe moves. Clean chains, fast roads and run medals give you additional goals without extra score or Coins. Your historical personal best and existing Arcade Coin rewards remain in place.
