How to read these numbers

Dates use America/Chicago. Accounts means confirmed, non-anonymous accounts that still exist; signup dates use account creation time. Owner/test exclusions apply only to accounts explicitly marked by the operator. Unmarked test accounts can still appear.

New activity begins at the tracking date. Existing account and verified coin-purchase records may cover earlier dates. Client events can be missed offline or blocked, and repeated events are capped at 120 per account per hour. No chat text, email addresses, or player lists are shown.
