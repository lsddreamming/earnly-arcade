SURVIVAL FIELD MANUAL

Take them apart.

  1. Move left. Turn right. Use the left stick to move, and hold either side of the right turn pad to keep spinning. You can also swipe sideways to aim. Your robot faces where you look. Keep moving and cut across an exposed side to remove a limb. Change Camera in Pause for a wide, far, or close view.
  2. Watch the orange attack fan. A robot is winding up. Its raised blade and floor warning show where the strike is coming. Dodge out, or tap block just before impact for a parry.
  3. Jump, then Strike to chop down. Airborne strikes always swing overhead toward the ground. Face your target and strike while coming down. On the ground, hold Strike for a side cut, low leg sweep, then an overhead chop. Dodge through danger using energy.
  4. Break the machine. Missing legs slow robots down. Missing arms take weapons away. Break the head or core to finish them.
  5. Rebuild between waves. Pick one upgrade. Every two waves you transfer to the next arena. Every fifth wave brings the Crucible giant.

Desktop: WASD · mouse/drag to aim · click strike · Q block · Shift dodge · Space jump · Esc pause.
