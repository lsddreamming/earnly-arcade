Harbor Getaway

3:00ON FOOT Find the marked getaway car.
HARBOR DISTRICT / NIGHT SHIFT

After hours at the harbor

Grab the mint coupe, break the patrol’s line of sight, then drive it back to the garage. You have three minutes.

Drag the game screen to run. Lift to stop.

Drag up to drive or sideways to turn. Drag down to brake or reverse. Lift to coast.

Use WASD or arrows to move and drive, Q/R or mouse drag to look, E to enter or exit, Space for handbrake, and Esc to pause.
